Privacy Policy

4sh · Effective date: 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

The 4sh app ("the app") is made and run by Samudra Dasgupta, an individual developer in India ("we", "us").

This app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any school board, exam body or educational institution.

2. In short

  • No sign-up and no account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app.
  • No ads and no tracking. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.
  • The app sends only what it needs to work, to stay secure and to fix crashes. Each item is listed below.

3. What the app sends, and why

Security check. Before the app loads content, Google Play and Firebase App Check confirm it is the genuine app, installed from Google Play, on a genuine phone. This uses a short-lived security token. We use it only to decide whether to serve the request, and we never store it.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends a report: what went wrong (with personal text masked), your phone's model, its Android version, the app's version, and random IDs for this installation of the app. These IDs do not contain your name or contact details. We use crash reports only to fix problems. Google keeps a crash report for 90 days. Google keeps the installation ID until we delete it.

Purchases. Google Play handles all payments. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. When you buy, the app sends us the purchase's token from Google Play. We check it with Google, then keep the order number, the dates of your paid month and a scrambled form of the token (a one-way hash) that cannot be turned back into the token. We use these to unlock paid videos and books, to show "Your months" in Settings, and to help with support and refunds. We keep them for one year after your paid month ends, then delete them.

Access codes. If you enter an access code, our server checks it. Codes are not linked to any person.

Videos and books. Our server, run on Cloudflare, delivers videos, books and pictures to the app. Like any website, it handles your phone's IP address to deliver them, and to limit how often codes can be tried. We do not store IP addresses or locations, and we keep no record, linked to you, of what you watch or read.

Server logs. Our server keeps short technical notes of errors and refused requests, such as "no security token". They contain no IP address and nothing that identifies you. We keep them for up to one year, for security.

Emails to support. If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, your message and any order number you include. We use them only to reply and to help you. We keep support emails for about one year, then delete them.

4. What stays on your phone

Downloaded books, the last page you read in each book, your purchase status and any access code you entered stay on your phone. If your phone's backup is on, Android may back up the last page read to your own Google account. Nothing else from the app is backed up, and none of this is sent to us.

5. Who handles data for us

  • Google (Google Play, Firebase App Check, Firebase Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore): security checks, crash reports, purchase checks and storing purchase records. Purchase records are stored in India (Mumbai).
  • Cloudflare: our server and the storage for videos, books and pictures.
  • Hostinger: our support mailbox, stored in the European Union.

Some of this data may be processed outside India. We share data with no one else, except when the law requires it.

6. Children and teenagers

The app is for students aged 13 and over, their teachers and parents, and adults. If you are under 18, use the app only with your parent's or guardian's consent. A parent or guardian may ask us to delete a child's data (section 7). The app is not meant for children under 13.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Because there are no accounts, include your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") so we can find your purchase records. You can find it in the app under Settings → Your months, in the Google Play app under your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or in the receipt email Google sent when you paid. Crash reports and server logs are not linked to any name or order, so we cannot find them for one person; crash reports are deleted after 90 days anyway.

Write to support@4sh.education. We reply within one month.

8. Security

The app connects to our server and to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Purchase tokens and access codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Only our server and the developer can reach the stored records.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here and in the app, with a new effective date. If a change would use your data for a new purpose, the app will ask for your agreement first.
