Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30th September 2026

4sh Foundation is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to the Sokratyx App and to every website operated by 4sh Foundation, including 4shfoundation.org and its family of subject-specific learning sites (together, the "Services"). It outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 App Account Information (Sokratyx App only): when you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name and email address.

1.2 Contact Information (4sh websites): our websites do not have data-collection forms. If you click a "Chat on WhatsApp" link or copy an email address to write to us, you leave our website and contact us directly through WhatsApp or your own email client. Any information you choose to include in that message — such as your name or what you're asking about — is sent to us there, not collected through the website itself.

1.3 Usage Information:

  • Interactions with the App's and our websites' features.
  • Device information, such as IP address and browser type, gathered via analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and improve the App and our websites.
  • To personalise your experience and suggest relevant content.
  • To respond to messages you send us and communicate updates and new features.
  • To comply with legal requirements.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4sh does not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers for operational purposes (e.g. hosting, analytics).
  • Legal authorities if required by law.

4. Data Retention

Personal data is retained as long as necessary to provide the Services or comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request corrections to inaccuracies.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal and operational constraints.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • The App and our websites use cookies and analytics tools to enhance user experience and analyse usage patterns.
  • You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The App and our websites may include links to third-party services, including WhatsApp. 4sh is not responsible for their privacy practices.

8. Data Security

4sh employs industry-standard security measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

4sh does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

4sh reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy. Continued use of the App or our websites constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
