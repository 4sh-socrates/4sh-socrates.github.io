Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2 November 2026

This policy covers 4shfoundation.org and the 4sh learning websites that link to it. The 4sh app has its own Privacy Policy.

1. What we collect

Our websites have no sign-up and no forms. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device, and your approximate location; Google handles your IP address to do this. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or Google's opt-out add-on.

Our hosting providers handle your IP address to deliver the pages.

If you use a WhatsApp or email link to write to us, you leave our website, and your message reaches us through WhatsApp or your email service.

We do not sell your data.

2. Contact and Grievance Officer

Samudra Dasgupta, Grievance Officer · support@4sh.education. We answer questions and complaints within one month.
